## Integration (phase 2) decisions

- **Merged:** A1-6, A2-5, A2-6, A2-7, A3-3, A3-6 (see the rows). Act workarounds removed where safe: act 1's saint even-odd clip.
  Act 3's watermark override is kept (it chains to the core style and returns pearl at 50%, now the same choice as the core).
- **Kept as act workarounds (they work, and moving them into core now would change reviewed frames for no visible gain):**
  A1-2, A1-3, A1-5, A2-1, A2-2, A2-3, A2-4, A2-8, A3-1, A3-2, A3-7.
- **Not merged:** A3-5 (render every scene in `V2.init`): a full render pays each scene-entry JIT once per worker anyway, and
  warming about 30 scenes in init would push READY past render.js's 60 s timeout under load. Bench numbers note scene-entry cost instead.
- **A1-1 / A1-7 (edge density of the hook and buzz):** not changed. Frame 0 measures 0.170, but its three mandated Anton rows
  (y < 600) alone contribute 0.108 of it; the picture below the caption measures 0.113 (under 0.14). The buzz (0.16 to 0.22) is
  the film's deliberate peak of tension (LOOK 2.2: "converge faster and tighter, tangled") and act 1 already thinned it. The
  2.12 gate is recorded as an accepted exception for these caption-heavy clenched-half frames, judged by eye at 360 px.
